Production system

Nucleus Brain

Demo / Site

Question

Coordinate a large research or engineering workload across specialist AI sub-agents without losing evaluation or governance.

Role

Architect.

Method

AI orchestrator routing work across specialist sub-agents over MCP (Model Context Protocol): tool-calling workflows, a rubric-based evaluation loop scoring agent output on defined criteria, and human-in-the-loop review at each stage.

Division of work

Human

System architecture, evaluation-rubric design, and governance model.

AI

Sub-agents perform the delegated work under orchestration and evaluation.

Verification and limitations

Production system in active use across AGFarms ventures. Orchestration and evaluation infrastructure does not by itself guarantee the scientific or factual correctness of any downstream output; that still requires human review, applied per artifact.
