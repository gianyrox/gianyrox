Research Focus

I am looking for a research role, in a PhD program or on a research team at a company, working on one question: how should AI be designed and built into human learning, knowledge work, and scientific reasoning so that gains in machine capability produce gains in human capability? I work on it now at Bucket Foundation. Research OS for K‑12 is an educational knowledge graph and research environment built for education reform: it routes learners from questions through prerequisites and evidence toward producing new knowledge. It is the sandbox for this research, where I test how AI assistance changes what learners understand and can do on their own. The work on this site is the foundation: research infrastructure at 12.2M rows, working papers, open machine-learning tools benchmarked against published baselines, and production AI systems that keep a human in the loop.
